## About Me

I'm a Computer Science student from the Philippines who enjoys building things at the intersection of **design and code**.

I care a lot about making interfaces feel complete: spacing, motion, layout, flow, responsiveness, and whether someone can understand what to do without fighting the page.

Frontend and UI/UX are the parts of software that I enjoy most because they turn logic into something people can actually touch, read, use, and feel.

My current goal is simple:

> Build clean, intentional, and accessible digital experiences while continuously improving as a frontend/UI/UX developer.

---

## What I Like Building

- Interfaces that feel fun without becoming confusing
- Responsive layouts that work across screen sizes
- UI systems with reusable components
- Web experiences with motion, polish, and personality
- Projects where design decisions and code decisions support each other
